Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. The short version

Minute Muse is a static website. It has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels. Everything you write or configure is stored in your own browser and never transmitted to us.

2. What is stored, and where

Your tasks, journal notes, saved passages, session history and preferences are kept in your browser’s localStorage, under keys beginning with mm.. This data is yours: it lives on your device, is readable only by this site on this browser, and disappears when you clear site data. You can export a copy or erase everything from Settings → Your data.

  • mm.settings — appearance, session lengths, ambience levels
  • mm.tasks — your session task list
  • mm.notes — daily journal entries
  • mm.bookmarks — passages you have saved
  • mm.history — completed and partial focus sessions
  • mm.timer — the state of a session in progress, so a refresh doesn’t lose it

3. Location & climate

Minute Muse does not request your GPS location, and it cannot see your IP address from the browser. The climate badge makes a rough guess (tropical, northern or southern, winter or summer) from your browser’s timezone name. That guess is made entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere. You can override or disable it in Settings.

4. Third parties the page may contact

  • Unsplash — background photographs are loaded from Unsplash’s image CDN, as their API guidelines require. Unsplash may see the usual request metadata (IP address, user agent). See Unsplash’s privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — the two typefaces are served by Google’s font CDN. See Google’s privacy policy.

Ambient audio and the literary collection are served from this site itself. No third-party media players are embedded.

5. Notifications

If you switch on desktop notifications, your browser asks for permission. Minute Muse uses it only to tell you a focus phase has ended. You can revoke it at any time in your browser’s site settings.

6. Children

Minute Muse is suitable for general audiences and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date above will change with it. Because there is no account system, there is nothing to notify you about by email.

8. Contact

Questions or concerns can be raised through the project’s issue tracker.
