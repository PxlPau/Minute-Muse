Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Privacy-First Commitment

Minute Muse is architected with strict client-side privacy. We do not run tracking cookies, analytics trackers, or user profiling algorithms.

2. Local Storage (On-Device Persistence)

To retain your study workspace across page refreshes, Minute Muse uses your browser's built-in localStorage API. The following keys are stored exclusively on your device:

  • minuteMuseJournal: The text you write in your personal reflection journal.
  • minuteMuseAdminLocation: Your manual climate/season override preference (if chosen).

This information is never transmitted to our servers or third parties. Clearing your browser data or site storage will erase these entries immediately.

3. Location & Timezone Processing

Minute Muse does not access your device GPS or IP-geolocation APIs. The climate badge checks your browser's local timezone string (e.g. Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone) purely inside client-side JavaScript to estimate hemisphere and season.

4. Third-Party Integrations

When loading assets, standard browser requests are made to:

  • Unsplash (Images): Photography is hotlinked directly from Unsplash CDNs so photographers receive view attribution. Refer to Unsplash's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: Typography stylesheets (Inter and Playfair Display) are served via Google's font CDN.
  • Spotify (Optional): If you choose to open the Spotify widget, the embedded iframe connects directly to Spotify. No Spotify user information is collected by Minute Muse.
