    Credits & licences

    This room is built from other people’s generous work.

    Literature

    Passages are drawn from the open-source Literature Clock collection, a community index of lines from books that mention a specific time. Copyright remains with the authors and publishers; single excerpts are shown here in a transformative, educational context. Rights holders can request removal through the takedown process.
