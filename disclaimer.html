3. Literary Content & Fair Use

Literary excerpts are drawn from a curated, offline-filtered copy of the open-source Literature Clock dataset (originally compiled by JohannesNE based on Guardian readers' submissions). Literary copyrights remain with the original authors and respective publishers.

Minute Muse displays these single-sentence excerpts in transformative, educational context to indicate the current time of day. If you are an author or publisher requesting the removal of a specific excerpt, refer to the takedown process in our Terms of Service.
